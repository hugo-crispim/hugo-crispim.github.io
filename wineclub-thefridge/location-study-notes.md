### Evening rendering prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit / architectural concept visualization.
Asset: a polished photorealistic exterior concept rendering for The Fridge's proposal website.
Edit target is image 1, the wide photograph IMG_9043.jpeg. Image 2, IMG_9044.jpeg, is a supporting close-up of exactly the same frontage. Preserve the real building, camera perspective, upper-floor pale sage facade and all architectural ornament, the three existing large street-level windows with their white horizontal frames, the single narrow entrance door at far right, grey ground-floor piers, the mature tree on the left, low pale fence, mosaic Prague pavement, and red street plaque. Do not change building massing, window count, dimensions, sill heights, entrance position, or extend the premises.
Imagine only a restrained reversible fit-out of this real ground-floor unit as THE FRIDGE, a neighbourhood wine shop and intimate listening bar. Through the three windows show an inviting warm interior: olive plaster, a communal warm oak table with burgundy chairs, warm black pendant lights, and a continuous wall of tall WIDE glass-front temperature-controlled wine display cabinets with softly lit bottles against the interior back wall. Keep windows clear enough to see the wine shop. Add a modest elegant warm-cream 'THE FRIDGE' wordmark above the central storefront windows, fitting entirely within the existing grey fascia without covering any window. No other new text.
Lighting: quiet early autumn evening, still enough natural light to read the actual facade, warm interior glow, natural restrained reflections. Palette coordinates with a cream-paper, dark forest-green and burgundy editorial website. Remove the cardboard, construction clutter and dirt around the shop. Keep the autumn tree, architecture, railing and pavement accurate; no fantasy terrace, no outdoor seating or structures, no crowd, no added glazing or enlarged windows. Beautiful believable architecture photograph, understated hospitality, not glossy luxury or neon. Landscape 4:3 framing matching the target photo. No borders, no caption, no watermark.
